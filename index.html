How to play
  • Goal: move all 52 cards to the foundations. Each suit has two foundations — an up pile (A,2,3…) and a down pile (K,Q,J…). They build toward each other and meet in the middle; you choose where each suit splits.
  • The catch: the up and down halves of a suit can never cross. A 7 or 8 has no home until the ends climb to reach it — plan around it.
  • Tableau: build in alternating colors, one rank at a time, in either direction — a pile can run down (red on black) or up. Pick a direction when you start a pile; you can't reverse it partway. Move a tidy run as a group if you have enough free cells + empty columns.
  • Free cells: 3 single-card parking spots.
  • Controls: tap a card to pick it up, tap where it should go. Double-tap auto-moves a card: to a foundation if it fits, otherwise onto another card, then an empty column, then a free cell. Everything is face-up — it's pure skill.
